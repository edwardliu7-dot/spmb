---
name: SPMB waiting list matching
description: The named waiting-list reservation and AI review boundary for future SPMB changes.
---

Active waiting-list names reserve quota, but a submitted application never removes one automatically. Matching is a review suggestion only; the administrator must confirm the same student before the reservation is deleted, or reject the pair to suppress that suggestion later.

**Why:** Names from manual WhatsApp lists can be abbreviated or spelled differently, so automatic deletion risks releasing a seat or losing the reservation for the wrong child.

**How to apply:** Keep external AI input limited to name, level, and gender. Do not send NIK, phone numbers, addresses, or other family data to the matcher; preserve the human confirmation step for every removal.
