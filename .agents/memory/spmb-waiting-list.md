---
name: SPMB waiting list matching
description: The named waiting-list reservation and AI review boundary for future SPMB changes.
---

Active waiting-list names reserve quota. The public form may issue a short-lived, signed access token when the submitted name, level, and gender match an active booking; the token lets that reserved student submit even when the quota appears full. A submitted application still never removes the reservation automatically: the administrator must confirm the same student before the reservation is deleted, or reject the pair to suppress that suggestion later.

**Why:** Names from manual WhatsApp lists can be abbreviated or spelled differently. Unlocking only the matched student fixes the blocked-registration problem without releasing a seat or losing the reservation for the wrong child.

**How to apply:** Keep external AI input limited to name, level, and gender. Do not send NIK, phone numbers, addresses, or other family data to the matcher; validate the signed token again during submission and preserve the human confirmation step for every removal.
